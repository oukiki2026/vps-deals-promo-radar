What this site does today

This is a static, public AI guide site with a separate section for verified hosting offers. You do not need an account to read it. The site has no registration, contact form, payment processing or newsletter. We display labelled systeme.io and Admitad affiliate links, including an official Notta banner hosted on this site. No third-party advertising script or remotely hosted advertiser image is installed. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate page views, visits, referral hosts, device categories and loading performance. Its browser beacon does not use cookies or browser storage to track individual visitors. These metrics can be sampled or blocked and do not identify every person or provide a full click history. Optional Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose to allow it. We store your analytics preference in local storage; when allowed, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies.

Analytics

Cloudflare receives browser performance measurements and page/referral information through its analytics beacon. We review aggregate reports to improve this site, without keeping individual visitor IP records or session recordings. See Cloudflare Web Analytics data practices. Analytics begins when enabled and cannot reconstruct earlier browser visits.

Optional Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page views and engagement on kikomono.com. It is off until you select Allow analytics. Declining does not affect access to our guides. You can change your choice with Analytics preferences in the page footer; withdrawing consent stops collection and removes this site's Google Analytics cookies.

After consent, Google receives page paths, a sanitized referral origin, browser and device information, and technical request information to produce reports. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish browsers. We remove query strings and fragments from the page URL sent to Analytics, do not send names, email addresses or account details, and have not enabled enhanced measurement, Google Signals or advertising personalization in this integration. Reports are incomplete where visitors decline or block collection; earlier visits cannot be reconstructed. Cloudflare's separate aggregate analytics continues as described above.

After analytics consent, following an existing affiliate advertisement can also send an affiliate_click event with its merchant name, placement label and this page path. We do not send the affiliate tracking URL or identifier in that event. Clicks are not purchases or confirmed commission; revenue remains subject to the affiliate provider’s own records. Declining analytics disables this site’s click events.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Our preference record is kept for six months before asking again. Choosing analytics does not authorize personalized advertising.

Tutorial controls and practice files

Copy buttons copy only the displayed practice text to your clipboard when you click them; they do not send it to an AI service. Download links provide the fictional practice files shown in the guide. Exercise checkboxes are held in the current page only and reset on reload. These controls do not send your marks to us or store them in your browser.

The installed statistics integrations are Cloudflare Web Analytics and optional Google Analytics 4, as described above. This version does not load a self-hosted Umami statistics script.

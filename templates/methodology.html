OUR METHOD

Useful facts.
Visible limits.

A conservative offer monitor built on official public pages.

Sources and verification

We check robots.txt before fetching configured official sources. We do not access private dashboards, bypass anti-bot protections or infer prices from search snippets.

A record requires a real plan name, unambiguous current price, currency, billing conditions and official URL. Duplicate or conflicting records are discarded. Providers without a supported, verifiable price stay listed without a price.

Freshness and expiry

Scheduled checks are requested every six hours; execution can be delayed by the hosting platforms. Each record shows its actual retrieval time. Records older than $freshness hours are excluded during a build. Source failures remove that provider's offers from the next successful deployment. If deployment itself stops, the old snapshot remains online with its retrieval time visible.

We never invent an end date. A disclosed expired offer is removed. Unknown expiry does not mean guaranteed continuing availability.

Billing and comparison

Introductory rates may cover only part of a contract. Renewal, taxes and total charges can differ. We do not compare them as full-term costs or claim to find the cheapest provider.

Affiliate disclosure

No affiliate commission is configured at launch. Links go directly to the official sources. If an approved affiliate link is added later, its page will disclose that we may earn a commission and mark the link as sponsored. Rankings are not for sale.

Open records

The public dataset includes sources, retrieval timestamps, parser identifiers and source hashes. The repository contains the deterministic update and build code. Structured data does not guarantee search rankings or rich results.
